    Questions

    The company brain, explained.

    What is a company brain?

    A company brain is one shared memory of what your team knows: who owns what, what was decided and why. Calla builds it from your calls, so the knowledge stays even when people are busy or move on.

    How is Calla different from AI meeting notes?

    Meeting notes end when the meeting ends. Calla connects every call to the ones before it, so your team can ask questions across all of them and see the exact moment each answer came from.

    Does it work with my video call app?

    Calla follows the call on your own computer, so it works alongside the call app you already use. Nobody has to install anything or invite a bot.
