Boring Notch — private Apple Silicon package

1. Open “Boring Notch.app” from this disk image.
2. If macOS blocks it, try opening it once, then go to System Settings →
   Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. This is the only approval needed:
   the installed copy and every tool inside it are cleared at install time.
3. Choose Begin setup. The window shows each step, what it is doing right
   now, and download progress. After the first step it closes and the
   installed app in Applications opens its own setup window to finish —
   so every macOS prompt belongs to that one app.

Setup installs Boring Notch, AGY, Tangit, Codex and Claude, signs you in to
Google, downloads the speech model, asks for microphone, system audio and
calendar access, runs a short meeting test, and creates your first Tangit
profile. The Gateway step is optional.

You need macOS 15 or later, an Apple Silicon Mac, an internet connection,
your Google login, and at least 8 GB free. No Homebrew, Python, Node, Go or
Xcode is required. If Git is missing, setup opens Apple's Command Line Tools
installer and waits for it.

CALLS
Start a call from the notch and pick the meeting type: Standup, Sprint
planning, Refinement, Sprint review or Retro (team rituals); Design review
or Incident review (engineering); General, 1:1, Interview, Sales or Support
(conversations). Transcription stays on the Mac; selected transcript text
goes to your AI account.

IF SETUP STOPS
Reopen Boring Notch and choose Resume setup. Finished steps are kept and not
repeated — Google sign-in is not reopened once it works.

UPDATES
Open the next private DMG's Boring Notch.app; it updates in place and keeps
your accounts, settings, history and models. End any active call and quit
Tangit first.

LATER
Boring Notch → Setup, Repair & Updates…, or in a new Terminal window:
  boring-notch doctor
  boring-notch repair
  boring-notch update /path/to/new-package.dmg
  tangit

This is a private locally signed build, not an Apple-notarized release.
